# Thresholds

Thresholds are written before any result is computed and are not changed afterwards.

| Test | Metric | Pass | Conditional | Fail |
|------|--------|------|-------------|------|
| M4 PSI | PSI | < 0.10 | 0.10 to 0.25 | > 0.25 |

Note: 0.10 and 0.25 are industry practice, not regulatory requirements.
